# Product context

Legacy Senior Advocates is a concierge senior advocacy business serving seniors and their families. Its public website explains advocacy services and the founder's experience, helps visitors request a consultation, and links prospective advocates to the staff portal application.

## Audience and outcomes
Visitors include seniors, family caregivers, prospective advocates, and members of the press. Visitors need readable, reassuring information, clear navigation, and direct routes to consultation or contact. The Press page features verified independent coverage and lets visitors read the original reporting. Future coverage can be added as additional articles without empty placeholders.

## Platform and constraints
This is an existing static HTML website with inline CSS and JavaScript, hosted on Netlify. Forms use existing serverless endpoints; this press addition does not change them. The current website identity is governed by BRAND.md: navy and warm light backgrounds, Playfair Display headings, Inter body text, and the existing homepage header, navigation, and full logo on the Press page, as explicitly requested by the user. Minimum body size is 18px, new content controls have at least 44px targets; the incumbent header retains its established compact styling, and keyboard focus must remain visible.

## Confirmed press direction
Feature the Florida Weekly story published October 19, 2026, with room for future coverage. Use the existing brand and an unchanged local Dobbins family photograph. Link to the publisher rather than reproducing the article. Highlight the five panelists and moderator named in the article. The user explicitly requested the same full header as the homepage: logo, fixed navigation, link labels, blue consultation button, and hamburger overlay. This supersedes the earlier custom header direction for this page. No fabricated endorsements, coverage, or upcoming-event claims.
