# Product context

Legacy Senior Advocates is a concierge senior advocacy business serving seniors and their families. Its public website explains advocacy services and the founder's experience, helps visitors request a consultation, and links prospective advocates to the staff portal application.

## Audience and outcomes
Visitors include seniors, family caregivers, prospective advocates, and members of the press. Visitors need readable, reassuring information, clear navigation, and direct routes to consultation or contact. The Press page features verified independent coverage and lets visitors read the original reporting. Future coverage can be added as additional articles without empty placeholders.

## Platform and constraints
This is an existing static HTML website with inline CSS and JavaScript, hosted on Netlify. Forms use existing serverless endpoints; this press addition does not change them. The current website identity is governed by BRAND.md: navy and warm light backgrounds, Playfair Display headings, Inter body text, and a wordmark-only treatment on new surfaces. Minimum body size is 18px, controls have at least 44px targets, and keyboard focus must remain visible.

## Confirmed press direction
Feature the Florida Weekly story published October 19, 2026, with room for future coverage. Use the existing brand and an unchanged local Dobbins family photograph. Link to the publisher rather than reproducing the article. No fabricated endorsements, coverage, or upcoming-event claims.
