Advocate & Client App: the options, the costs, and why

Prepared 2026-07-23 · Decision due Aug 1 · All prices verified against vendor pages on 2026-07-23

Advocates need five things on their phone, with clients' health information handled safely (every tool touching health details needs a signed BAA — a contract where the vendor legally commits to protecting it). Budget matters: 1–5 advocates, no paying for unused features.

Visit transcriptionrecord a doctor visit, transcript lands in the client's file
Time clockclock in/out around appointments
Shared calendarclient + advocate + family all see appointments
Team chatask the whole advocate team a question mid-visit
Client portalone place per client for transcripts & documents

Recommendation

Route A — Google Workspace Business Standard + Clockify Free: $28/mo for 2 advocates, one BAA, tools advocates already know.

The deciding insight: moving email off free Gmail to Google Workspace is the #1 HIPAA fix required no matter what — free Gmail and the current client spreadsheet have no BAA available at any price. Since every route includes Workspace anyway, the question is really: what do we pay on top of it? Route A's answer is "almost nothing," because that same subscription already includes the calendar, chat, file storage, and (via Google's Gemini AI, covered by the same BAA) transcription.

The three viable routes

Recommended

  • Still needs Workspace on top — Carepatron doesn't do email

Considered and ruled out — and why

SimplePractice$173 / $395 per moAccount owner is documented as a clinician role — a non-licensed advocacy business may not even be able to sign up. Highest eligibility risk of the group; don't spend more time unless a test signup succeeds.
IntakeQ$114.90 / $204.90 per moNo transcription feature at all — the biggest single requirement gap — plus no time clock and unconfirmed family access.
Practice Better$155 / $305 per moBuilt around coaching programs; internal team-broadcast chat and family calendar both unconfirmed. More platform than the team needs at a higher price than Carepatron.
Spruce Health$48 / $120 per moExcellent HIPAA chat/texting and the best mobile app — but no calendar, no document portal, and its transcription only covers calls placed through Spruce. Covers 1½ of 5 needs. Kept on file as the upgrade if LSA later wants to text clients directly.
Home-care agency suitesAxisCare, WellSky, CareSmartz360Purpose-built for shift-based caregiving with real time clocks and family portals — but sales-quoted pricing with a realistic floor of $200–500/mo, and still no visit transcription. Wrong business model fit.
Specialist AI medical scribesAbridge, Freed, Nabla, Heidi, OtterAll disqualified: terms are written for treating clinicians documenting their own patients (not an accompanying advocate), and/or BAAs are gated behind enterprise-only sales contracts. This is why transcription comes from Gemini or the platform's scribe instead.

What would change the answer

Clients/families find the Drive folder too bare during the pilotGo hybrid: keep Workspace as the backbone, add Carepatron for its portal only (+$62/mo at 2 advocates). This is the designed fallback, decided by pilot feedback — not a restart.
Gemini's segmented transcription proves too clumsy in practiceTrial Carepatron's scribe via the hybrid, or price Otter Enterprise (custom quote, second BAA). Costs nothing to try Gemini first.
Vendor maturity matters more than priceChoose Healthie — the most battle-tested and best-documented platform, at ~8× Route A's cost.
Nothing — this happens on every routeWorkspace migration (the #1 HIPAA fix), the free AWS BAA + storage audit, and stopping the Supabase health-data mirror (making it compliant would cost ~$600–950+/mo — not worth it at this size).

Before anything goes live

  1. 30-day pilot (Route A): Week 1 — sign up, sign the Google BAA, migrate email + client spreadsheet, set up Clockify with client codes. Week 2 — Pat + one advocate run a full mock appointment: clock in → consent line → record in segments → Gemini transcript into the client's Drive folder → shared calendar entry a test "family member" can actually see → team-chat question → clock out. Weeks 3–4 — one real consenting client, then go/no-go (or hybrid).
  2. Attorney, item 1: confirm LSA's status (working for families, it's most likely not HIPAA-regulated at all — we run to HIPAA standards as a business choice and for liability).
  3. Attorney, item 2: Florida is an all-party consent state (Fla. Stat. § 934.03 — felony exposure). The recording-consent script must be settled before any advocate records a visit, on any route.
