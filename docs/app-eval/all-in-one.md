# All-in-One HIPAA Platform Research (Option 1)

**Prepared:** 2026-07-23 | **For:** Legacy Senior Advocate (LSA) — Task 10 of the LSA Rebrand + HIPAA App Evaluation plan

## Method

Researched via web search + official vendor pricing/help-center pages, retrieved 2026-07-23. Every price and capability claim below is cited with a source URL. Where a vendor's marketing page doesn't spell out a detail (e.g., exact BAA tier, whether a feature is a true broadcast team channel vs. 1:1 messaging), that is flagged explicitly as unverified/needs-a-live-test rather than assumed.

**Context reminder:** LSA advocates are **not licensed clinicians** and have **no NPI**. They accompany clients to doctor visits, coordinate care, and talk to family — they do not diagnose, treat, or bill insurance. Two deal-breakers to watch for every candidate:
1. Does the platform require a licensed/credentialed account owner to sign up?
2. Can a **family member** (not just the client) see the shared calendar/portal — not just get email notifications?

**Important nuance on "transcription" for all six candidates:** every AI scribe/AI-charting feature found is built to transcribe **the platform's own session** (a Zoom/telehealth call or an in-person encounter logged inside that vendor's app). None of the vendors advertise "record a doctor's-office visit that happens outside our platform, then attach the transcript to the client's chart" as a named workflow. In practice the fallback — record on the advocate's phone with any recorder app, then **upload the audio/transcript file as a document** to the client's chart via the portal's file-upload feature — works on every platform that has document storage, regardless of whether the vendor's own AI scribe can be pointed at an external recording. Scorecards below distinguish "AI scribe exists" (✅/⚠️) from "will transcribe an externally-recorded doctor visit" (⚠️ at best, unverified — needs a pilot test) and separately confirm document-upload-as-workaround.

## Healthie

- **Pricing (2 advocates / 5 advocates, monthly):** $199.99 / $349.99 — Group plan (base $149.99/mo covers 1 provider seat + 1 support-staff seat, additional provider seats $50/mo each), retrieved 2026-07-23. Sources: [gethealthie.com/pricing](https://www.gethealthie.com/pricing), [help.gethealthie.com/article/773-healthie-pricing](https://help.gethealthie.com/article/773-healthie-pricing). *(Assumes both advocates need full provider-level access; if one could operate as the included "support" seat, 2-advocate cost drops to $149.99.)*
- **BAA:** Yes — included on all paid plans. Source: [gethealthie.com/baa](https://www.gethealthie.com/baa), retrieved 2026-07-23.
- **Capability coverage:** transcription ⚠️ (AI Charting add-on, Plus/Group tiers only, "additional fees apply" — built for sessions conducted in Healthie's own telehealth/in-person flow; unverified for an externally-recorded doctor visit) | time clock ❌ (no staff clock-in/hours feature found — only appointment scheduling) | shared family calendar ⚠️ (client accounts can be **linked** to indicate a relationship and a linked family member can get their own portal access, but the help doc says this is "currently available upon request" for Plus plans and above — not self-serve, needs a support ticket to enable) | team group chat ✅ (confirmed: internal group chat/video for organization members, no clients included — [help.gethealthie.com/article/168](https://help.gethealthie.com/article/168-create-an-internal-group-chat-for-members-of-your-organization)) | client portal ✅ (all tiers)
- **Gaps needing an add-on app:** dedicated time-clock/hours tool (e.g., Homebase, Clockify); confirm the family-portal-access support-ticket workflow before committing.
- **Non-clinical advocacy business eligible:** Yes — Healthie is widely used by health/wellness coaches and other non-licensed practitioners; no NPI or license verification gate found at signup.
- **Training burden (1=easy, 5=hard):** 3 — full-featured EHR-style platform (forms, billing, telehealth, programs) with more surface area than a 1-5 person advocacy team needs; core workflows (chat, portal, scheduling) are straightforward once configured.
- **Mobile app quality (advocate works from phone):** Separate provider and client apps; provider app supports internal chat and portal access from phone. Generally well-reviewed but is a full-practice app, not a lightweight single-purpose tool.

## SimplePractice

- **Pricing (2 advocates / 5 advocates, monthly):** $173 / $395 — **Plus** plan ($99/mo base) + additional clinician seats at $74/mo each (2-5 clinician practice pricing), retrieved 2026-07-23. Source: [simplepractice.com/pricing](https://www.simplepractice.com/pricing/).
- **BAA:** Included on all paid tiers — confirmed by SimplePractice's own BAA/Terms of Service support article (https://support.simplepractice.com/hc/en-us/articles/360018696052-SimplePractice-BAA-Terms-of-Service-and-Trust-Security-information, retrieved 2026-07-23). SimplePractice also displays HIPAA/HITRUST/PCI compliance badges platform-wide.
- **Capability coverage:** transcription ⚠️ (AI Note Taker add-on $17.50-35/mo, or Care Aide bundle $24.50-59/mo; same external-recording caveat as above) | time clock ❌ | shared family calendar ❌/⚠️ (no evidence found of general family-member calendar sharing — the Client Portal model is built around a single client, or a parent/guardian for a minor client, not general family access; unconfirmed either way) | team group chat ⚠️ (Secure Messaging with team members is available on Essential/Plus — [support.simplepractice.com](https://support.simplepractice.com/hc/en-us/articles/41870164930189-Using-Secure-Messaging-with-your-team) — but reads as 1:1/threaded messaging between staff, not a confirmed broadcast group channel) | client portal ✅
- **Gaps needing an add-on app:** time clock; family calendar sharing needs verification; team chat needs verification as a true broadcast channel vs. 1:1.
- **Non-clinical advocacy business eligible: NO / HIGH RISK — unresolved.** SimplePractice's Account Owner role is documented as "a clinician role," and the platform is historically built around licensed/certified health and mental-health professionals selecting a profession at signup. No documentation was found confirming that a non-licensed senior advocate (no license, no NPI) can create a primary account. Non-clinical **staff roles** (biller, scheduler) exist, but those appear to sit *under* a licensed account owner, not as the practice's primary business type. **This must be tested directly at signup before ruling SimplePractice in or out** — it's the single biggest eligibility risk of the six candidates.
- **Training burden (1=easy, 5=hard):** 3 — mature but feature-dense platform built for solo/group clinical private practice, not care-coordination teams.
- **Mobile app quality (advocate works from phone):** Well-regarded clinician and client mobile apps ("SimplePractice for Clinicians" / client portal app); among the most mature mobile experiences of the six.

## Additional candidates considered (not scored — cited for completeness)

Non-medical **home care agency** software — **AxisCare**, **WellSky Personal Care** (formerly ClearCare), **CareSmartz360** — was investigated as a strong conceptual fit: these platforms are purpose-built for non-clinical caregiving businesses and natively bundle Electronic Visit Verification (a real caregiver clock-in/out time clock), a **family portal**, scheduling/calendar, and two-way chat — all four of LSA's core needs except medical-visit transcription, out of the box. See [axiscare.com/pricing](https://axiscare.com/pricing/) (blocked WebFetch, 403), third-party estimate at [bridgecareos.com/best-home-care-software-2026](https://bridgecareos.com/best-home-care-software-2026/) ("$200-500/mo base + $10-25/caregiver/mo" for mid-market platforms like AxisCare), and [caresmartz360.com/pricing](https://www.caresmartz360.com/pricing/) ("starts at $10 per active client/month," typical small-agency budget "$195-500/mo"), all retrieved 2026-07-23.

**Not scored on the full scorecard because:** none of the three publishes transparent, self-serve per-seat pricing for a 1-5 person startup the way the six required candidates do — all require a sales quote, and third-party benchmarks put the realistic floor around $200-400/mo even before per-caregiver fees, which is likely **more expensive** than every candidate above except SimplePractice at 5 advocates. They also have no medical-visit transcription feature (their domain is caregiving-shift documentation, not appointment accompaniment), so LSA would still need a separate transcription tool regardless. Worth a revisit only if LSA's business model shifts toward high-volume, shift-based non-medical caregiving rather than appointment accompaniment/advocacy.

## Top 2 picks and why

**1. Carepatron ($62/mo for 2 advocates, $155/mo for 5, Plus tier at regular pricing)** — cheapest full-coverage option with a BAA on every tier including free (lowest financial risk while testing), the most complete capability match (team chat ✅, client portal ✅, family-linked portal ⚠️-but-present, AI scribe included), and the lowest training burden of the feature-complete platforms (rated 2/5). The two open questions to verify in a free-tier trial before committing: whether the family "relationship" link actually surfaces calendar visibility (not just documents/messages), and whether AI Scribe can be pointed at an externally-recorded doctor visit or whether LSA will rely on the document-upload workaround.

**2. Healthie ($199.99/mo for 2 advocates, $349.99/mo for 5, Group tier)** — costs roughly 3x Carepatron but has the most solidly *confirmed* capability set: internal group chat is explicitly documented (not inferred), client portal and BAA are unambiguous, and it's the most mature/battle-tested platform of the group (largest user base, most detailed help documentation found during research). Best choice if Carepatron's family-calendar or transcription-workaround questions don't resolve favorably in a trial, or if LSA wants a more established vendor for a HIPAA-sensitive workflow even at higher cost.

**Runner-up worth flagging, not a top-2 pick:** Spruce Health is the cheapest option ($48/2, $120/5) and by far the best mobile experience and lowest training burden, but it structurally covers only chat well — no calendar, no real portal, and transcription doesn't fit the actual use case. It would need to be paired with a separate calendar and portal tool, which starts to look like the best-of-breed stack (Task 11) rather than a true all-in-one.

**Do not pursue without further verification:** SimplePractice — its clinician-account-owner model creates real risk that a non-licensed advocacy business cannot even sign up as the primary account; this should be tested directly (attempt account creation, or call sales) before spending further evaluation time, regardless of its otherwise-solid feature set.
